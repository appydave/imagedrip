Brand: AppyDave. Warm, typographic, grid-aware. A thumbnail is a cropped extension of appydave.com — someone should land on the image, then the website, and feel they are the same place.

Palette. These occupy 80%+ of every frame:
  brown   #342d2d  a FIRST-CLASS ground colour, not merely an outline
  gold    #ccba9d
  yellow  #ffde59  reserved for ONE attention element, never decorative fill
  ground  #faf5ec near-white cream, or #25201e dark surface
  amber   #c8841a  sequence numerals only (01, 042) — never a focal element

At most ONE semantic accent per image (AI purple, success green, pain red, impact orange, doc blue), on a single small element. Most images use none at all.

Typography: Bebas Neue for display, Oswald for watermarks, labels and sequence numerals. Flat, clean, grid-aware composition. A subtle warm-cream paper texture is the only acceptable gradient.

Artifact: a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail PLATE, 1280x720, for the AppyDave channel.

A PLATE, not a finished thumbnail. The headline hook text is deliberately NOT drawn — it is composed later, at higher fidelity, by a separate step. What this image must deliver is the ground, the palette, the composition and the ghost watermark: everything the hook will later sit on top of. Leave clear, uncluttered space on the LEFT for it, occupying 30-60% of the frame width.

The ghost watermark is the single strongest tie to the web brand, and the only text this image carries:
  - ONE word, uppercase, Oswald or Bebas Neue, bold weight
  - brown #342d2d at 8-15% opacity on a cream ground, or cream #faf5ec at 6-12% on a dark brown ground
  - ALWAYS bleeds off at least one edge — never centred, never fully contained
  - sized 1.5x to 2.5x the cap height of the space left for the hook
  - sits behind everything else, never in front
  - exactly one per image

Composition — use the pattern named in the prompt:
  Type-Dominant   ground plus watermark alone, held open for a bold claim
  Type + Glyph    one small iconographic element (arrow, cursor, node, terminal prompt, pill) — the default
  Split Panel     a dark brown panel beside a cream panel — before/after, problem/fix
  Sequence Badge  a large amber numeral plus a topic word plus the watermark
  Ghost-Forward   the watermark IS the hero at 40%+ opacity, very large, everything else recedes

Hard constraints — never do any of these:
no headline text, no hook text, no large foreground lettering, no captions, no titles drawn in the image
no humans, no human faces, no hands
no halftone grain, no newsprint texture, no comic-book outlines
no 3D render, no photorealism, no neon colours
no gradients beyond a subtle warm-cream paper texture
no text smaller than roughly 40px cap height at 1280x720
no more than one semantic accent colour
no illustrated scenes with multiple characters or props — at most one glyph
no centred ghost watermark — it must bleed off an edge
no second competing watermark
no clutter — one idea per frame

Subject: the "12 Days of Claudmas" series on the AppyDave YouTube channel — twelve daily builds in December, each a working AI tool built live with Claude Code and vibe-coding techniques, assembling into one content-creator pipeline called FliGen.

Each prompt in this queue is one day of that series. It names the day, the topic, the composition pattern and the ghost watermark word. Where a prompt gives the published video TITLE, that is context for the design only — the title is never drawn in the image.
